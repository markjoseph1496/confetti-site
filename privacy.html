Confetti Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Confetti is a personal, non-commercial event photo-sharing app operated by Mark Joseph ("the operator"). Guests at an event scan a QR code and upload photos, which are stored in the event host's own Google Drive.

Information the app collects

  • Guest name — the name you type when joining an event. It is used only to label the folder your photos are stored in, so the hosts know who shared them.
  • Photos you choose to upload — stored in the event host's private Google Drive. Nothing is uploaded without you selecting it.
  • Basic technical data — standard server logs and IP-based rate limiting, used only to keep the service working and prevent abuse. No analytics or advertising trackers are used.

Use of Google user data

Confetti connects to Google Drive through Google's OAuth system using the drive.file scope, which means the app can only see and manage files and folders it created itself — it has no access to any other content in the connected Google account.

Google user data (the photos and folders the app creates in Drive) is used solely to provide the photo-sharing service described here. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train AI or machine-learning models, and never transferred to third parties except as required to provide the service or comply with law. Confetti's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where your data lives

Uploaded photos and guest names are stored only in the event host's Google Drive. The app itself keeps no database. Your browser stores a small amount of local data (your name for the event and a private upload credential) on your own device only.

Deleting your data

You can delete photos you uploaded from the event page on the device you registered with. You may also ask the operator or the event hosts to remove your photos or your name folder at any time.
