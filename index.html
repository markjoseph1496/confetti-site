Confetti

Every guest is a photographer. Scan a QR code at the event, share your photos — they land straight in the hosts' album.

  1. 1Scan the QR code on your table or invitation — no app, no account.
  2. 2Tell us your name, then complete photo missions or share any photos from your camera roll.
  3. 3Your photos go directly into the hosts' private Google Drive album, sorted by guest and mission.

For hosts

Confetti is a small self-hosted app. You create an event, print its QR code, and guests' photos arrive in your own Google Drive — organized into a folder per guest, with photo-mission subfolders. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
